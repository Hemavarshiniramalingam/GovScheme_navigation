2. Database Schema Specification (ERD Entities)

Model Name Key Attributes & Types Relationships Functional Role
CitizenProfile id, age_group, education_level, occupation, state, income_bracket, specific_category 1:N with ApplicationRecord, 1:N with NotificationLog Persists normalized intake parameters across citizen exploration.
Scheme code, name, ministry, level, sector, legal_description, plain_language_summary, benefit_highlight, deadline 1:N with EligibilityRule, 1:N with DocumentRequirement, 1:N with ApplicationRecord Central repository of statutory provisions & plain citizen guides.
EligibilityRule rule_field, operator, expected_values, legal_clause_text, plain_language_rule, is_mandatory N:1 with Scheme Atomic rule criteria evaluated by the AST engine.
DocumentRequirement document_name, why_required, issuing_authority, is_mandatory, digilocker_available N:1 with Scheme Checklist generator with issuance authorities & DigiLocker readiness.
ApplicationRecord application_number, current_status, status_summary, official_portal_ref, benefit_amount N:1 with Scheme, 1:N with StatusHistory Unique lifecycle tracking token for 5-stage progress monitoring.
StatusHistory stage, title, officer_remarks, action_required_note, updated_by, timestamp N:1 with ApplicationRecord Audit trail logging every desk verification remark and stage step.
NotificationLog notification_type, channel, title, message, action_url, is_read, created_at N:1 with CitizenProfile & ApplicationRecord Multi-channel alert record (SMS, WhatsApp, Email, In-App).

3. Value Matrix: How GovScheme Navigator Differs from Traditional Government Portals

Feature Dimension Traditional Portals (myScheme / State Portals) GovScheme Navigator Approach
Language & Clarity Complex, legalistic gazette text and bureaucratic jargon (Flesch score < 35). NLP Legal-to-Plain translation with Flesch Readability > 75 (Grade 6 comprehension).
Eligibility Verification Users must read multi-page PDF guidelines manually to verify if they qualify. Deterministic AST Matching Engine gives exact "Why you qualify" reasoning and match score.
Lifecycle Tracking Redirects out to disconnected department portals with separate logins and forgotten tokens. Unified 5-Stage Stepper with real-time audit remarks, token lookup, and action-required alerts.
Citizen Notifications No proactive push alerts; citizens miss crucial deadlines and document upload requests. Event-driven notification bus across In-App drawer, WhatsApp API, SMS, and Email.
